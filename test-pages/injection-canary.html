What is a token approval?

A token approval lets a contract spend a set amount of your tokens. You sign it once, and the contract can then move up to that amount.

You can revoke approvals at any time from the Approvals tab. Revoking costs a small amount of gas.

Unlimited approvals are convenient but riskier: if the contract is hacked, everything of that token you hold can be taken.
